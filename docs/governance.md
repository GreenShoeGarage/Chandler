# Governance

Automation may normalize text, extract attributes, form candidate clusters, and propose compatibility. It may not publish a canonical family or approve a Class A or Class B mechanical substitution.

Every review decision records reviewer, timestamp, reason, affected evidence, rule path, and catalog release. Rejection, ambiguity, safety concerns, deferral, merge, split, evidence requests, and reassignment remain visible in the audit history.

The public lifecycle is Suggested → Data Checked → Bench Tested → Recommended → Watch → Retired. A record may move directly to Rejected with a reason.
